About

Hi, I'm April

I am a mom, a wellness enthusiast, and someone who got tired of trusting labels without reading them.

My journey started around 2019, not because of a single dramatic moment, but because my health was telling me something needed to change. I started asking questions, doing research, and slowly pulling back the curtain on the products, foods, and materials that had been part of my everyday life for years. By 2020 it had grown into something bigger, a real shift in the way our whole family lives and the choices we make for our home.

Then I became a mom, and everything I had already learned felt even more important.

I am not a doctor or a nutritionist. I am a working mom doing my best to build a home that is genuinely healthy, not just marketing-label healthy. I have two rescue dogs, a deep love for animals, and a heart for helping other families find their way to a cleaner, simpler life.

That is the soul behind Simple Holistic Lifestyle. And the thing I want you to know most is this: it is not about being perfect. It never has been. Perfection is exhausting and honestly not the point. This is about making one better choice today than you made yesterday. Swapping one thing at a time. Letting small, loving decisions compound into a healthier life without the overwhelm of trying to change everything at once.

I share the products we actually use, the research behind why we use them, and the honest truth about what is worth the switch and what is not. Everything you see here has earned a place in our home first.

If you are just starting your non-toxic journey, welcome. Start small, stay curious, and know that every single swap counts, for you, for your family, and for the world you are creating around you.

What We Believe In

We believe your home should be your sanctuary. We believe clean living does not have to be complicated or expensive. We believe in quality over quantity, real ingredients over clever marketing, and making today just a little better than yesterday.
